About

I'm a software developer based in Alberta, Canada. I work across the stack — distributed backends, machine-learning and retrieval pipelines, and bare-metal embedded firmware — and I'm happiest on problems that cross those boundaries.

The projects below are all things I wanted to exist and then built end to end: ASP.NET Core and Python services, local and hosted LLM pipelines, a ten-process auction-hunting system, embedded C on an 8-bit microcontroller. The throughline is systems that are honest about their own limits — explicit pipeline stages, visible failure modes, and design decisions I made for reasons I can explain.

Open to new opportunities — email is the best way to reach me.
