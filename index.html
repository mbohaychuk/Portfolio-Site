01 / About

About

I'm a software developer based in Alberta, Canada. I work across the stack: distributed backends, machine-learning and retrieval pipelines, and bare-metal embedded firmware. I'm happiest on problems that cross those boundaries.

The projects below are all things I wanted to exist and then built end to end: ASP.NET Core and Python services, local and hosted LLM pipelines, a thirteen-process auction-hunting system, embedded C on an 8-bit microcontroller. What they have in common is that they don't hide their limits: pipeline stages are explicit, failure modes are visible, and I can explain every design decision I made.
